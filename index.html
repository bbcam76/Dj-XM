About the Series
A cinematic rise through pressure, loyalty, and power.

MBI is a gritty cinematic story about ambition, street survival, and the dangerous climb toward influence. It explores what happens when hunger meets opportunity and when power becomes both the dream and the trap.

Built as a prestige franchise world, MBI blends premium visual storytelling with emotionally charged character arcs, giving it the energy of a modern street epic with blockbuster branding potential.
